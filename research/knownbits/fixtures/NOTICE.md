# Third-party research fixtures

NiceToMeetYou is the work of Xuanyu Peng, Dominic Kennedy, Yuyou Fan,
Ben Greenman, John Regehr, and Loris D’Antoni.

The 39 corpus files are unmodified solution.mlir files extracted from the
MIT-licensed artifact https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17371668 .
The two helper files are unmodified files from https://github.com/Hatsunespica/xdsl-smt
at commit 4ec509edab067d62c00f76778789170061ae299c, paths
`tests/synth/KnownBits/meet.mlir` and `tests/synth/KnownBits/top.mlir`.
The upstream MIT copyright notice is retained in LICENSE_MIT.txt.

not claim upstream endorsement, full C++ equivalence, or upstream defects on
the basis of refuting stronger obligations.
