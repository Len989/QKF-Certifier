\section{Further questions}

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\arabic*)]
\item \textbf{Compact presentation optimization.} Proposition~\ref{prop:flattening} settles existence of a zero-overhead finite ground extension. How many derived equations, or how many distinct subterms, are needed to achieve a given improvement? Which transformations of universal presentations preserve either asymptotic visibility class?
\item \textbf{Fixed-signature spectra.} The Catalan construction uses fresh constants as independent gadgets. Which profiles survive when the signature, number of constants, or number of equations is fixed in advance?
\item \textbf{The gap between proof formats.} How large can $\lambdarw_E(s,t)-\lambda_E(s,t)$ be under restrictions on arity, contexts, or relation shape? For action chains, what invariant describes congruence visibility itself beyond the upper bound by the sequential envelope?
\item \textbf{Secondary certificate objectives.} Once minimum term depth is fixed, what is the cost of optimizing proof size or support cardinality? How small can a lower-threshold separating model be, compared with the canonical DAG-class construction?
\item \textbf{Other context geometries.} Ordinary one-sorted action contexts yield $H(s)(n)=n+\max_{1\le m\le n}(s(m)-m)$ for sequential rewrites. Which envelope operators arise under different sort restrictions or other explicitly specified proof resources?
\end{enumerate}
