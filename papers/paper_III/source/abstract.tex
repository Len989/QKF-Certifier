\begin{abstract}
QKF studies how incomplete observations determine computations through forced rows, their kernels, and compatible quotients. This paper develops that viewpoint into a certificate architecture for word programs. A consumer specifies which distinctions a proof must retain; checked native facts label the observation classes; algebraic closure completes the required rows; and a finite closure certificate composes their actions across an arbitrary number of bit positions. The resulting interface can describe the action of an expression without reconstructing every intermediate word.

We retain the exact finite relation-matrix kernel and the complete nine-row characterization of the coordinatewise KnownBits fragment, and extend the account to contextual observations, guarded whole-word reconstruction, and signed mask--interval carriers. A descending repair pass uses a common-prefix observation with 49 certificate states; an ascending successor pass factors three physical phases into two carry observations and has an eight-state certificate. The upper helper computes an exact masked maximum with an explicit empty-case distinction. The lower helper preserves the joint carrier under a stated sign condition and need not return its exact minimum.

A self-contained Lean 4 pilot checks forced-row rules, the carry factor, finite gluing, and the successor result for every positive word width. Its scope is the explicit mathematical model and the modeled nonsign repair loop. The Python checkers, complete Java procedures, and full Graal constructor remain separate proof obligations. Research evidence includes 11 of 39 complete Nice to Meet You programs, 104 of 411 components, and two of five complete Graal mask results; these denominators describe different experiments. The release separates the coordinatewise CLI, broader research capsules, and Lean project, with exact source manifests and replay instructions.
\end{abstract}
